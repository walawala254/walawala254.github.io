Services

Risk, fraud, compliance, and analytics support for fintech teams that need clarity fast.

I combine analytical thinking with payments-risk context to help teams monitor behavior, review merchants, explain risk, and improve operational decisions.

Transaction Monitoring & Fraud Rule Design

Design practical rules around velocity, unusual behavior, merchant activity, BIN/card/IP signals, and rule-hit investigation queues.

Outputs: rule logic, scenarios, alert notes

Merchant Risk Review & KYB Support

Review merchant profiles, websites, business models, licensing signals, prohibited activity indicators, and onboarding risk narratives.

Outputs: review framework, risk memo, checklist

AML/CFT Risk Controls & Typology Mapping

Map risk scenarios across customers, merchants, geography, payment methods, velocity, sanctions exposure, and suspicious behavior.

Chargeback, Dispute & Scheme Rule Analytics

Compliance Documentation & Technical Writing

Write policies, SOPs, fraud prevention manuals, regulator-facing notes, process flows, and internal risk documentation.

Outputs: SOPs, manuals, process maps

PSP / FinTech Operational Risk Support

Support payment operations with clearer risk workflows, escalation paths, evidence standards, and decision documentation.

Outputs: workflow design, control checklist

Web Risk Screening & Merchant Intelligence

Review merchant websites for trust gaps, licensing issues, business model inconsistencies, vertical risk, and suspicious signals.

Outputs: screening template, risk summary
