About Dave

I sit in the useful middle: data, payments risk, fraud signals, and compliance reality.

I started from data analysis and data science: asking better questions, finding patterns, and turning raw information into decisions. Today, I apply that thinking to payments, fraud, AML/CFT, merchant risk, transaction monitoring, and fintech operations.

I am not just a data person and not just a compliance person. I work where payment behavior, merchant risk, fraud signals, regulatory expectations, and messy operational data meet.

How I work

What I Do

I help teams design monitoring logic, review merchant risk, interpret fraud patterns, build dashboards, and document controls in a way that supports real decisions.

How I Think

I like clear definitions, clean evidence, practical controls, and explanations that both technical and non-technical teams can trust.

How I Communicate

Risk work is only useful when people can act on it. I focus on plain-language documentation, strong review notes, and clean reporting.
